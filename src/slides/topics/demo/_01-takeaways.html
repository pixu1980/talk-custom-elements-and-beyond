What the demo really proves?

  • The store is the truth box, and every component keeps reading from the same Proxy tree
  • store:change is the bell that wakes each custom element's render(state) after every mutation
  • <for> and <if> are not cute helpers; they are the practical fingers that touch lists and conditionals all day long
  • A tiny runtime can power a surprisingly rich interface, but the real product question is still how much of that runtime you want to own yourself
